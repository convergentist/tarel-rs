\section{Problem definition}
\label{sec:problem}

Let $\mathcal{C} = \{c_1, \ldots, c_M\}$ be a population of
\emph{instruments}, each $c_m$ an (exchange, symbol) pair. For each
$c_m$ and each clock $\kappa \in \{\text{phys}, \text{evt},
\text{info}\}$ we observe a stream of LOB delta updates that, when
replayed, produces state vectors $\{x^{(m,\kappa)}_t\}$ partitioned
into buckets $B^{(m,\kappa)}_1, \ldots, B^{(m,\kappa)}_{K_{\kappa
m}}$. For each instrument $c_m$ we compute two scalars: a
\emph{Stationarity Score} $S^{\text{stat}}_m \in [0,1]$ derived from
RQA statistics on the buckets, and a \emph{Stability Score}
$S^{\text{stab}}_m \in [0,1]$ derived from $J$-seed W-GAN-GP training
on the recurrence-plot tensors of those buckets. The central testable
claim of the paper is
\begin{equation}
H_1:\quad \mathrm{Spearman}\,\rho\!\left(S^{\text{stat}},\,
S^{\text{stab}}\right) > 0
\label{eq:h1}
\end{equation}
across the $M$-instrument population, with one-sided alternative and a
$B = 10{,}000$-iteration label-permutation null. We make three
explicit assumptions, each of which is unusual enough to highlight
here: (i) within a 60-minute parquet window the LOB delta stream is
locally sufficient to reconstruct full top-25 snapshots; (ii)
per-bucket distance-plot geometry is a meaningful summary of
microstructure regime; (iii) the $J$-seed sample of W-GAN-GP training
behavior is informative about the conditioning of the underlying data
distribution. The first assumption forbids cross-window state-carry;
the second is the fingerprint conjecture in operational form; the
third is the assumption that gives the bridge correlation
in~\eqref{eq:h1} its scientific content.
