\blockhead{ioEdge}{Block 1 --- Latent input
$z \in \mathbb{R}^{128 \times 1 \times 1}$}

The latent vector $z$ is drawn from an isotropic Gaussian
$\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{I}_{128})$ with $d_z = 128$. It is
presented to the network not as a flat 128-vector but as a degenerate
3-D tensor with shape $128 \times 1 \times 1$, i.e.\ 128 channels at a
$1 \times 1$ spatial grid. This reshape is a notational convenience that
lets the first stage be written as a transposed convolution rather than a
linear projection followed by a reshape; the two are arithmetically
equivalent when the kernel and output spatial size both equal four and
stride is one. The choice of an isotropic Gaussian prior is standard for
W-GAN-GP: the Kantorovich--Rubinstein duality used to derive the
gradient-penalty objective requires only that the prior have a tractable
density and full support on its support set, and
$\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{I}_{128})$ satisfies both.
