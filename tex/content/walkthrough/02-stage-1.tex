\blockhead{opEdge}{Block 2 --- Stage 1 operator
$\mathrm{ConvT}^{128 \to 512}_{k=4,\,s=1,\,p=0} \circ \mathrm{BN} \circ
\sigma_{\mathrm{ReLU}}$}

Stage 1 is the only stage that does \emph{not} double the spatial
resolution; its job is to lift the latent vector into a small but
high-channel spatial grid. The transposed convolution
$W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{128 \times 512 \times 4 \times 4}$ with stride 1 and
zero padding expands the $1 \times 1$ spatial extent of $z$ to
$4 \times 4$ while contracting the channel count from a high latent
dimensionality (128) to an even higher feature-channel count (512).
Intuitively, the network is buying \emph{spatial expressiveness} at the
cost of \emph{channel expressiveness}; subsequent stages reverse this
trade. The output is then passed through batch normalization
$\mathrm{BN}_1(\cdot; \gamma_1, \beta_1)$, which centres and scales each
channel across the mini-batch, and a pointwise ReLU activation
$\sigma_{\mathrm{ReLU}}(x) = \max(0, x)$.
