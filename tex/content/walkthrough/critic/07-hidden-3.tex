\blockhead{stateEdge}{Block 7 --- Hidden state
$\mathbf{h}^{(3)} \in \mathbb{R}^{256 \times 8 \times 8}$}

Third internal feature map, $256 \cdot 8 \cdot 8 = 16\,384$ scalars. By
this depth, the network has compressed the input recurrence plot down
to an $8 \times 8$ spatial summary encoded in 256 channels. The
information-theoretic intuition is that a $1 \times 64 \times 64$
binary-or-near-binary recurrence plot has, by construction, only a
modest amount of resolvable structure (its target recurrence rate is
$5\%$, so most pixels carry redundant information), and a $256 \times
8 \times 8$ feature map has more than enough capacity to summarise that
structure while remaining compact enough to score globally. The
critic's later stages compress this representation further into a
scalar.
