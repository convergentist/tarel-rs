\blockhead{opEdge}{Block 8 --- Stage 4 operator
$\mathrm{Conv}^{256 \to 512}_{k=4,\,s=2,\,p=1} \circ
\sigma_{\mathrm{LReLU}}(0.2)$}

The penultimate downsampling stage. Channel doubling $256 \to 512$ and
spatial halving $8 \to 4$ continue the canonical pattern. This is the
last stage that uses the stride-2, padding-1 configuration; the
terminal stage that follows is structurally different (kernel 4, stride
1, padding 0) because its job is no longer to downsample but to
\emph{collapse} a $4 \times 4 \times 512$ feature volume into a single
scalar. Stage 4 leaves behind a feature map whose spatial extent
matches the kernel size of the next stage --- by design --- so the
terminal $4 \times 4$ convolution receives exactly one position's
worth of input at a time and is, in effect, a per-position linear
projection.
