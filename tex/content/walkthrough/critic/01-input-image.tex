\blockhead{ioEdge}{Block 1 --- Input image
$\mathbf{x} \in [-1, 1]^{\,1 \times 64 \times 64}$}

The input to the critic is a single-channel $64 \times 64$ tensor whose
per-pixel values are bounded in $[-1, 1]$. During training, this tensor
comes from one of two sources: a \emph{real} batch sampled uniformly
from the training tensor bank (a per-bucket min--max-normalised
recurrence plot persisted by Stage~2 of the pipeline), or a \emph{fake}
batch produced by the generator $G_\theta(z)$ for fresh
$z \sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{I}_{128})$. Both occupy the same
support $[-1, 1]^{1 \times 64 \times 64}$ by construction; the critic
itself is never told which source produced a given input. That symmetry
is what makes the Wasserstein-1 estimator
$\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{x} \sim \mathbb{P}_r}[D_\phi(\mathbf{x})] -
\mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{x} \sim \mathbb{P}_g}[D_\phi(\mathbf{x})]$ a
meaningful quantity: each expectation is computed against $D_\phi$'s
\emph{undiscriminating} scoring of inputs from the two distributions.
