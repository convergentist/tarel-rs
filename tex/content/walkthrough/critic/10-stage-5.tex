\blockhead{opEdge}{Block 10 --- Stage 5 operator
$\mathrm{Conv}^{512 \to 1}_{k=4,\,s=1,\,p=0}$ (no activation)}

The terminal stage of the critic differs from the previous four in
three respects: (i) the channel count collapses from 512 to 1, matching
the scalar nature of the Wasserstein score; (ii) the kernel size
matches the spatial extent of $\mathbf{h}^{(4)}$, so the stride-1
zero-padded convolution collapses the $4 \times 4 \times 512$ volume to
a $1 \times 1 \times 1$ scalar in a single step; (iii) there is no
activation function on the output. The absence of a final activation is
deliberate. A sigmoid would constrain the output to $(0, 1)$, which is
fine for the classic GAN binary-classification objective but
incompatible with the Wasserstein-1 dual formulation: the
Kantorovich--Rubinstein duality requires
$D_\phi(\mathbf{x}) \in \mathbb{R}$, not $D_\phi(\mathbf{x}) \in (0,
1)$. A tanh would similarly bound the score and prevent the gradient
penalty from being computed against a genuine unbounded scalar field.
Leaving the output raw lets the difference of expectations
$\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{P}_r}[D_\phi] - \mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{P}_g}[D_\phi]$
approximate $W_1$ directly.
