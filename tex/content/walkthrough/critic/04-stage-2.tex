\blockhead{opEdge}{Block 4 --- Stage 2 operator
$\mathrm{Conv}^{64 \to 128}_{k=4,\,s=2,\,p=1} \circ
\sigma_{\mathrm{LReLU}}(0.2)$}

Stage 2 sets the canonical downsampling pattern that the next two
stages will repeat: stride 2, padding 1, kernel 4, channel doubling.
Each spatial dimension halves ($32 \to 16$); the channel count doubles
($64 \to 128$). This budgeted trade --- spatial expressiveness for
channel expressiveness --- is the exact inverse of the generator's
channel-halving, spatial-doubling pattern, and is what gives the
critic-generator pair its overall topological symmetry. The $(4, 2, 1)$
configuration is preferred over $(3, 2, 1)$ for the same
checkerboard-avoidance reason that motivated the generator's choice: at
stride 2, kernel 4, padding 1, every output cell sees exactly two input
cells, with no overlap-induced amplification or attenuation that would
introduce systematic spatial bias.
