\blockhead{opEdge}{Block 2 --- Stage 1 operator
$\mathrm{Conv}^{1 \to 64}_{k=4,\,s=2,\,p=1} \circ
\sigma_{\mathrm{LReLU}}(0.2)$}

Stage 1 is the structural dual of the generator's terminal stage: a
stride-2 convolution with kernel size 4 and padding 1 that halves the
spatial extent ($64 \to 32$) while expanding the channel count from 1 to
64. The kernel tensor $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 64 \times 4 \times
4}$ sees, at each output position, a $4 \times 4$ patch of the input
image, and the stride-2 step guarantees that consecutive output cells
tile the input with the same kind of evenly-spaced receptive-field
overlap that makes the generator's transposed convolutions
artefact-free. The activation is a leaky-ReLU with negative slope
$0.2$: $\sigma_{\mathrm{LReLU}}(x) = \max(0.2\,x,\,x)$. The leakiness
matters here because the critic is updated five times per generator
step under the locked $n_{\mathrm{critic}} = 5$ schedule, so any dead
neurons that emerge during early training would cripple the inner
loop's effectiveness. There is, deliberately, \emph{no} batch
normalisation in any stage of the critic: batch normalisation
introduces cross-sample dependence inside each mini-batch, which
invalidates the i.i.d.\ assumption of the per-sample gradient penalty
that enforces $D_\phi$'s 1-Lipschitz property.
