\section*{Parameter accounting}

A complete count of the learnable parameters in $D_\phi$ illustrates
the budget choices implicit in the architecture. The five convolutions
contribute, by stage,
$1 \cdot 64 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 1\,024$,
$64 \cdot 128 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 131\,072$,
$128 \cdot 256 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 524\,288$,
$256 \cdot 512 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 2\,097\,152$, and
$512 \cdot 1 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 8\,192$ weight scalars respectively
(plus a small bias per output channel, omitted here). There are no
batch-normalisation parameters because BN is absent throughout. The
total is roughly $2.76$~million learnable parameters --- approximately
$73\%$ of the generator's $\sim 3.80$~million. Stage~4 dominates the
budget because both its input and output channel counts are large, the
mirror of the generator's Stage~2 dominance. This back-loaded
allocation of capacity is intentional: the early critic stages perform
geometric reduction whose statistics are largely a function of local
image structure, while the deeper stages aggregate that structure into
a global verdict and therefore need more capacity. The
generator-critic parameter ratio of roughly $1.4$ to $1$ is a typical
W-GAN-GP balance; pushing the critic too small relative to the
generator leads to the so-called ``mode collapse onto the critic's
blind spots'' failure mode, while a critic that is too large relative
to the generator wastes training budget on a Lipschitz constraint that
is already tight.
