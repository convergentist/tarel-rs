\blockhead{stateEdge}{Block 9 --- Hidden state
$\mathbf{h}^{(4)} \in \mathbb{R}^{64 \times 32 \times 32}$}

The fourth and final internal feature map, $64 \cdot 32 \cdot 32 =
65\,536$ scalars. The activations at this layer have the same spatial
extent as a half-resolution recurrence plot and 64 channels of feature
information. Empirically, this is where most of the high-frequency
texture of the target recurrence plot is encoded; the final stage's
role is mainly to project this rich feature representation down to one
channel and apply the squashing nonlinearity.
