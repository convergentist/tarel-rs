\blockhead{stateEdge}{Block 5 --- Hidden state
$\mathbf{h}^{(2)} \in \mathbb{R}^{256 \times 8 \times 8}$}

This is the second internal feature map, with $256 \cdot 8 \cdot 8 =
16\,384$ scalars. The shape doubling is the first instance of the
\emph{progressive-upsampling invariant} that holds throughout the rest
of the network: each subsequent stage takes the previous spatial extent
and doubles it while halving the channel count, keeping the total tensor
volume roughly constant in this layer ($16\,384$ vs.\ $8\,192$ in
$\mathbf{h}^{(1)}$, growing by exactly a factor of two; the same factor
will recur). At this resolution the network begins to commit to
medium-scale recurrence-plot structures such as diagonal bands and
rectangular regimes.
