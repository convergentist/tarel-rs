\blockhead{ioEdge}{Block 11 --- Generated sample
$G_\theta(z) \in [-1, 1]^{\,1 \times 64 \times 64}$}

The output is a single-channel image of size $64 \times 64$ with
per-pixel values in $[-1, 1]$. Multiplying by the persisted
$(\min, \max)$ tuple of any training bucket inverts the per-bucket
normalisation and recovers a distance-plot value with the same units as
the original Euclidean pairwise distance. The output passes downstream
into three independent post-hoc analyses without further
parameterisation: (i) the critic $D_\phi$, which scores it during
training; (ii) the mode-coverage nearest-neighbour search, which
compares it against the training tensor bank at each $5{,}000$-step
checkpoint; and (iii) optional visual inspection or downstream regime
classification, which can use $G_\theta(z)$ as a synthetic
recurrence-plot data point.
